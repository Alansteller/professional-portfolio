GOOD QUESTION

Wondering how
it all works?

Tap a question. No jargon—just clear answers before you decide.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. Tell me about your business and what you want customers to see. I'll guide you through the decisions in plain language.

What happens after I contact you?

We'll discuss your goals, the content and features you need, then agree on scope, price, timeline, and revisions before work begins.

Will it work on a phone?

Yes. The layout is designed to adapt to phone, tablet, and desktop screens. We'll check the pages on common screen sizes before launch.

Do I need to buy a domain?

Not always at first. A static site can be published on a free GitHub Pages address. A custom domain is optional and its registration and renewal cost extra.

Can my website accept payments or bookings?

Those features may be possible, but they need extra planning and may require paid services or backend systems. We'll discuss the options and cost before including them.
