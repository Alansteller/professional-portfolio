GOOD QUESTION

Before you ask… we probably have an answer.

Do I need to know coding? ＋

No. Tell us about your business and what you want your website to do. We'll explain the options in plain language.

Will my website work on mobile phones? ＋

Mobile-friendly layout is part of the plan. We'll check common screen sizes before delivery.

Do I need to buy a domain? ＋

A custom domain is optional and may have a separate yearly cost. Free static hosting can be suitable for some simple websites.

Can customers pay or book directly on the website? ＋

Basic contact links are straightforward. Online payments, live bookings, user accounts or databases need extra planning and may require paid services or a backend.
